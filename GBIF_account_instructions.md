## Creating a GBIF Account

First, navigate to <a href="https://www.gbif.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">gbif.org</a> and click the <strong>Login</strong> button in the upper right corner:

<img src="https://sunray1.github.io/2025-05-09-GBIF-NA-DNAPublishing/fig/GBIFHome.png" width="900"/>

Then choose your preferred method of registering your account:

<img src="https://sunray1.github.io/2025-05-09-GBIF-NA-DNAPublishing/fig/GBIF_login_options.png" width="900"/>

That's it.
